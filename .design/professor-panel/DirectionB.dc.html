Two independent draws without replacement

An urn holds 7 red and 5 blue marbles. Two marbles are drawn one after another without replacement. What is the probability that the second marble is blue, given that the first was red?

5/12
5/11 answer key
7/12
35/132
Worked solution
After a red is removed, 11 marbles remain and 5 of them are blue, so P(blue second | red first) = 5/11.
